# Good Company Demo Club handbook

Fictional demonstration material. These are not JDI policies or real events.

## Helping at an event
Arrive fifteen minutes before the time on your confirmed volunteer assignment.
The event coordinator will introduce you to your station partner. If your plans
change, tell the coordinator; you do not need to share a personal explanation.

## Community dinner
Please RSVP using the contact and deadline in the event notice. Share dietary
needs privately with the coordinator. Do not post another member's information
in the group chat.

## When details are missing
The published calendar provides dates and times. The event notice supplies
attire, what to bring, and meal details. If they disagree, ask the coordinator
which is current before making plans.
