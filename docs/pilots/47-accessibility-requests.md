# Accessibility request pilot decision (#47)

Decision: no-go pending a named pilot's intake and fulfillment process. Message
format preferences do not establish an accommodation service or authorize collecting
medical details. No sensitive intake, promises or implementation issues are created.

Required evidence: responsible fulfillment owner; permitted request categories;
participant consent and withdrawal route; minimum operational details; private
access roles; response deadlines; authoritative confirmation of actual arrangements;
retention/deletion schedule and exception escalation. Prefer a requested arrangement
and contact route over diagnoses or medical histories.

A bounded workflow could track requested, acknowledged, arranged, verified and
unresolved states, each with its source and responsible owner. Only a verified
provider/venue confirmation may mark an arrangement fulfilled; a sent reminder is
not an accommodation. Keep the module optional and separate from general rosters.
Do not build it until the organization supplies these concrete requirements.
