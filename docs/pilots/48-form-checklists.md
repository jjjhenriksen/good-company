# Form checklists: status aid for a Bethel 337 candidate pilot

Refs #48. Decision: **conditional go for a status-only prototype; no live reminders**.

The owner's existing Bethel 337 context makes JDI's public form catalog a concrete
discovery source. [JDI resources](https://jobsdaughtersinternational.org/resources/)
lists the annual Bethel report (110), personal health form (125a), and release,
waiver and consent form (125b). This inventory does not establish which documents
are required for a particular local event.

[JDI Youth Protection](https://jobsdaughtersinternational.org/youth-protection/)
states that CAV applications must follow training and be submitted within six months
of its date; background-check completion also affects training validity.
This is a published relative rule, not a known deadline for a named person.
An authorized administrator must provide the actual dates and current requirements.

## Candidate scope

Track whether an owner-supplied item is missing, reported submitted, acknowledged
by its authoritative status source, or withdrawn. A participant's report of submission
does not establish acceptance, valid certification or compliance.

| Candidate item | Status authority to name before enabling | Actual deadline currently supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual Bethel report 110 | Responsible secretary/report recipient | None |
| Event consent 125b | Event owner and designated document custodian | None |
| CAV application/training status | Authorized CAV administrator or registry | No individual training date |
| Personal health form 125a | Authorized document custodian | None; contents excluded |

The proposed MVP stores item ID, participant/organization reference, official form
link/version, source-provided due date with timezone, status, source ID and opaque
acknowledgment reference. Do not upload form contents, health data, signatures,
background-check results, identity documents or registry credentials.

## Authority, dates and retention

The custodian controls status acknowledgment; the participant can report submission
and ask for a correction. Good Company cannot certify eligibility, approve adults,
submit applications, or override the authoritative registry.
No deadline is calculated without a verified source date and the applicable rule.
Ambiguous month-end dates, rule revisions and conflicting sources require owner
resolution before scheduling reminders.

Only the participant and authorized custodian can view an individual's checklist.
Aggregate group notices contain counts only. The owner must separately authorize
recipients, reminder timing and budget. Choose a retention period before storing
personal checklist status; delete it after the event/application reconciliation period
and retain only the minimal agreed audit reference.

## Prototype acceptance and remaining discovery

Use fictional item IDs and explicit source dates. Show that a reminder neither marks
an item submitted nor certifies compliance; participant-reported submission remains
distinct from custodian acknowledgment; changed/withdrawn deadlines invalidate
queued reminders; unauthorized viewers get no individual status.

To enable a real pilot, supply its required items, actual deadlines, custodian, status
source access, retention duration and reminder remit. Do not create runtime integration
issues until those fields are concrete. Public source review date: 2026-10-01.
